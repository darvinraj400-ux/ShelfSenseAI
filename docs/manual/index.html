Part 1

Getting started

Everything you need to register, log in, and find your way around.

01

What you need

  • A smartphone, tablet, or computer
  • An internet connection
  • A web browser — Chrome, Safari, or Edge
  • For employees: an invitation email from your shop owner

How to open the app

  1. Open your web browser (Chrome, Safari, or Edge)
    Screenshot: Browser window open
  2. In the address bar, type: app.shelfsenseai.online
    Screenshot: URL typed in address bar
  3. Press Enter. The login page appears.
    Screenshot: Login page loaded
02

Register as an owner

New shop owners

If you're opening a new shop, this is where you start. You'll create an account, set up your shop, and become its Owner.

  1. From the login page, click the Register link at the bottom
    Screenshot: Register link on login page
  2. Choose "Create a new shop (become an Owner)"
    Screenshot: Two registration options shown
  3. Enter your shop name (e.g., "Kedai Runcit Pak Ali")
    Screenshot: Shop name field filled
  4. Select your state — this helps the app find local market prices
    Screenshot: State selector
  5. Optionally enter your district (e.g., "Segamat") for even more local pricing
    Screenshot: District field filled
  6. Enter your email address
    Screenshot: Email field filled
  7. Choose a password, then re-enter it in Confirm Password
    Screenshot: Password fields
  8. Click Create Account
    Screenshot: Create Account button
  9. Your shop is created. You're now the Owner and can log in.
    Screenshot: Success message / login page
Why state and district matter ShelfSenseAI uses your location to find the closest market prices. Choosing "Segamat" gives hyperlocal data; choosing only "Johor" gives state-wide data.
03

Join a shop (as employee)

New managers New staff

If your shop owner has invited you, this is how you set up your account and join the shop.

  1. From the login page, click the Register link
    Screenshot: Register link
  2. Choose "Join an existing shop"
    Screenshot: Join shop option selected
  3. Enter your email address — must match the one your owner invited
    Screenshot: Email field filled
  4. Choose a password, then re-enter it in Confirm Password
    Screenshot: Password fields
  5. Click Create Account
    Screenshot: Create Account button
  6. Log in with your new account
    Screenshot: Login page
  7. Go to Notifications from the main menu
    Screenshot: Notifications menu
  8. Find the invitation from your shop and click Accept
    Screenshot: Invitation with Accept button
  9. You're now part of the shop. The dashboard shows the shop's products.
    Screenshot: Dashboard after joining
Use the email your owner invited If your email doesn't match, you won't see the invitation. Ask your owner to re-invite you.

Part 2

Owner & Manager

Product management, pricing decisions, employee management, and market data health.

08

Adding a product

Owner Manager

Add a product to your shop's catalogue so you can track its stock, record its sales, and receive pricing recommendations.

  1. From the dashboard, click the Add Product button
    Screenshot: Add Product button on dashboard
  2. Enter the product name. As you type, official PriceCatcher item names may appear as suggestions — click one to auto-fill
    Screenshot: Product name with autocomplete suggestions
  3. Optionally enter the brand (e.g., MILO, MAGGI, or leave blank for generic items)
    Screenshot: Brand field filled
  4. Enter the cost price — what you paid the supplier for one unit
    Screenshot: Cost price field filled
  5. Enter your target margin in percent (e.g., 30 for 30% profit)
    Screenshot: Target margin field filled
  6. Verify the selling price — it auto-calculates from cost + margin, but you can adjust it
    Screenshot: Selling price auto-calculated
  7. Enter the quantity per package and unit (optional — e.g., 1 kg, 500 g, or 1 pcs). This describes one package's size, not your stock level
    Screenshot: Quantity and unit fields filled
  8. Tick "Government Price-Controlled Item (Barang Kawalan)" if this product falls under KPDN price control
    Screenshot: Barang Kawalan checkbox ticked
  9. Optionally enter a category (e.g., Snacks, Beverages) — or leave it for the AI to suggest later
    Screenshot: Category field filled
  10. Click Save. The product appears on your dashboard.
    Screenshot: Product saved, dashboard shows it
Match to PriceCatcher for market data If you pick an official PriceCatcher item name from the autocomplete, the app can match your product to real market prices and generate pricing recommendations.
Cost + margin = selling price Target margin is your goal, but the app enforces a minimum cost floor of cost × 1.05 so you never sell at a loss.
09

Pricing recommendations

Owner Manager
  1. On a product page, scroll to the Pricing Recommendation section
    Screenshot: Pricing Recommendation section
  2. Read the suggested price and its status (INCREASE, REDUCE, or MAINTAIN)
    Screenshot: Suggested price with status badge
  3. Review the safety limits applied (KPDN cap, cost floor, margin floor, market sanity, PCAPA)
    Screenshot: Guardrails breakdown
  4. Read the plain-English explanation of why this price was suggested
    Screenshot: AI explanation text
Nothing changes automatically Recommendations sit as PENDING until you decide. The app never changes a price on its own.

Applying or dismissing

Owner Manager

To apply a recommendation

  1. Click the green Apply button
    Screenshot: Apply button highlighted
  2. The selling price updates and a success message appears
    Screenshot: Success confirmation
  3. A new row appears in Price History
    Screenshot: Price history updated

To dismiss a recommendation

  1. Click the Dismiss button
    Screenshot: Dismiss button highlighted
  2. Choose a reason from the dropdown list
    Screenshot: Reason dropdown open
  3. The decision is logged as DISMISSED
    Screenshot: DISMISSED status shown

Part 4

Support

When something doesn't work as expected.

16

Troubleshooting

Can't log in?

  • Check your email and password
  • Contact your shop owner

Can't see the invitation?

  • Make sure your email matches the one your owner invited
  • Check that the invitation hasn't expired (48 hours)
  • Ask your owner to re-send the invitation

Page won't load?

  • Check your internet connection
  • Refresh the page
  • Try a different browser

App is slow?

  • Wait a few seconds and refresh
  • Check your internet speed

Can't see a button?

  • Your role may not have access to that feature
  • See "Who can do what" at the top of this manual
  • Ask your shop owner
